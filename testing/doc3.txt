In secure database systems, privacy protection is essential.
Encrypted indexes allow keyword queries without decrypting the full dataset.

Researchers analyze leakage in searchable encryption schemes.
Leakage occurs when an attacker can infer information from access patterns or query frequency.

Techniques such as padding, batching and epoch-based key rotation are used to mitigate these risks.

Experimental results often compare multiple configurations to evaluate the trade-off between security and efficiency.
